Drawshank Redemption used the lead they built on Friday and Saturday to hold off a hard charging Doe Boys squad behind clutch wins from Kevin Conway, Jeff Hull, and Jeffrey Javier. A hard fought first day on Friday at Del Monte golf course saw Drawshank staked to a small 2.5 to 1.5 lead. Saturday at Blackhorse saw a disaster for Doe Boys, as Kirk's Drawshank squad took 3.5 of the 4 available points to jump to a big 6 to 2 lead going into the decisive final round at Bayonet on Sunday.

Although the Doe Boys came out swinging, with Damon beating Ramon and Vince putting another epic story onto poor Fernando's match play resume (in front of his girl, no less - savage work there Vince), it was unfortunately too little, too late. Conway completed a clean sweep of his weekend by winning the 17th hole over Ted to ice his match at 2&1, cementing his 3-0-0 record and earning him tournament MVP honors as the only player to win all his matches. Jeffrey Javier won his epic, back-and-forth match with David Peterson on 18 after an absurd 17th hole themselves. Javier called his shot off the tee, executed it, and made the birdie putt - but the Oklahoma State alumnus wasn't phased as he answered tit for tat with his own birdie to extend the round to the 18th hole. What might have been the round of the day, however, was Jeff Hull fighting back from 5 down after just 8 holes, fending off all of the bullshit that Bayonet could possibly throw at him (you wouldn't believe some of the lies he had in the bunkers), and even staring down a chip-in from Pete Lopez on 14 that put Hull down 3 holes with just 4 to play. After which Jeff put his head down and won each of the remaining four holes to close out not only Pete, but the entire Doe Boys squad by securing the 8.5 points necessary to win the inaugural LTD Cup! Congratulations to Kirk and his band of merry men for securing the victory!
